Your affirmations should sound like you, not like a stock phrase.

Celeste turns your wishes, dreams, and words into a personal manifestation practice. You receive an Anchor Scene to read, an affirmation shaped by your answers, and a Bridge for Today: one small, concrete step within your control.

Choose the focus that fits this moment — love, prosperity, career, health, confidence, or peace — and describe it in your own words. The experience begins with what you shared instead of reducing your story to a generic collection of phrases.

A SCENE MADE FROM YOUR ANSWERS

Share what you want, what matters, and how you imagine that future. Celeste combines only relevant details to create a personal sensory narrative. You can see which elements were used and edit the title, affirmation, and Bridge for Today.

A RITUAL THAT FITS YOUR DAY

Return to what matters with the One-Minute Ritual. Read your affirmation, choose an intention, and move on without turning self-care into another heavy task.

FROM VISUALIZATION TO ACTION

Each Anchor Scene ends with a Bridge for Today: a short, possible action. Adapt it to your reality and record what happened, what did not, or what needs to change in Traces of Change.

DREAMS HANDLED WITH CARE

Share a dream, record how you felt when you woke up, and choose what it means to you. Celeste uses those elements to create a personal affirmation without symbol dictionaries, predictions, or diagnoses.

THREE STARS, A LIVING SCENE

Light the three stars of your Celestial Trace and your Anchor Scene comes alive: the stars become the sun, golden light spreads, and your affirmation appears over a scene inspired by your answers.

IN THE VOICE YOU CHOOSE

Pick a narration voice and hear your story, visions, and affirmations in it. With your permission, the voice is created with AI from your own text, only when you tap to listen, and it needs an internet connection. Offline, Celeste uses your phone's built-in voice.

TO HEAR WHEN YOU WAKE UP

In My wake-up, you build your own sequence: up to six items from your Anchor Scene, visions, and affirmations, in any order. At the time and on the days you choose, a reminder with sound arrives on your phone, sometimes a few minutes late. Open it, light the three stars, and the sequence plays in the voice you chose: no reading, no microphone, just listening.

YOUR JOURNEY, ONE DAY AT A TIME

In the Journey tab, follow your 21-day practice: your day streak, practices completed per day, and the milestones along the way. Your Traces of Change are there too: honest observations, even on the days when nothing changed. You can edit or delete each one.

TO SHARE

Share an affirmation, a vision or your Anchor Scene as a vertical 9:16 image or a short video, with Celeste's web address. Preview it first, then choose where to send it.

WITH CELESTE, YOU CAN:

- create a personalized Anchor Scene, story, and affirmation;
- hear your story and affirmations in the voice you choose;
- practice with a one-minute daily ritual;
- follow a personal 21-day journey;
- choose and edit your Bridge for Today;
- keep private Traces of Change;
- turn dream reflections into new affirmations;
- revisit your journey and favorite content;
- use the app in English or Portuguese.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL

Celeste shows which details were used for personalization. You can edit or delete your records and decide what to keep. No account is required, and personal content is never posted automatically.

If you choose to report generated content, Celeste tells you inside the app exactly what will be sent for review. Your original answers and dream report are not included in the report.

Celeste is a reflection and well-being tool. It does not predict events, replace therapy, or guarantee money, relationships, health, or any outcome.

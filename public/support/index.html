Support

Guidance for using Celeste, controlling your data and resolving common issues.

Updated August 31, 2026.

A public support email has not been provided yet. The Google Play account owner must provide a real address before distribution. This page already offers self-service help but cannot yet receive individual requests. No fictitious contact has been published.

Control or delete your data

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Under Privacy and data, read the policy and change optional cloud processing permissions.
  3. Delete manifestations, dreams or traces in their respective areas.
  4. Use Reset my journey to remove local practice data and restart onboarding.

This release does not create a personal account, so there is no remote account to delete. Clearing app/site data or uninstalling the app also removes the local copy according to the operating system's behavior.

Read the full Privacy policy.

Cloud personalization is not working

  • Confirm the device is connected to the internet.
  • Open Profile and check whether permission for that feature is enabled.
  • Scene, dream and narration permissions are separate; enabling one does not authorize the others.
  • Profiles marked Under 18 use only on-device alternatives.
  • If a provider is unavailable or a response cannot be used safely, Celeste keeps or creates the on-device alternative.

Voice or audio will not play

  • Check media volume and whether silent mode is interfering.
  • Press Play again; browsers may block automatic audio.
  • Try a narrator preview, which is bundled with the app and does not use the cloud.
  • For personal narration, confirm the separate voice permission in Profile and an internet connection.

A notification or alarm does not appear

  • These features depend on the installed app; the website cannot schedule a system alarm.
  • Open device settings and confirm notification permission.
  • Alarms depend on a compatible device and OS version and may require separate system authorization.
  • Battery-saving or manufacturer restrictions may delay reminders.

The app is stuck

  1. Fully close and reopen Celeste.
  2. Check free device storage and install available updates.
  3. As a last resort, use Reset my journey or clear app data. This deletes local practice data and cannot be undone.

Privacy request or content report

The public channel for individual requests will be the support email that the owner still needs to provide. Until it is published, use the local controls above to stop new uploads and delete on-device content.

On generated content, tap Report this AI content, choose a reason and submit the in-app form. Celeste sends the generated output, reason and optional note; it does not include the prompt, your original answers or the raw dream report.

Safety and emergencies

Celeste is a reflection and well-being tool, not an emergency service, and does not diagnose or treat conditions. If you are in a crisis or immediate danger, contact local emergency services and a qualified person or professional.

Never send a password, API key, financial credential or identity document in a support request.

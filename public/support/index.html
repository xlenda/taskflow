Support

Guidance for using Celeste, controlling your data and resolving common issues.

Updated September 4, 2026.

Support and privacy contact: suporte@celestegroup.biz. This address must remain active and be monitored by the team responsible for Celeste.

Control or delete your data

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Under Privacy and data, read the policy and change optional cloud processing permissions.
  3. Delete manifestations, dreams or traces in their respective areas.
  4. Use Delete submitted AI-content reports in Profile to remove remote rows linked to this installation's pseudonymous session.
  5. Use Reset my journey to remove local practice data and restart onboarding.
  6. In Journey, save a readable, unencrypted JSON backup. The installed app opens the system share sheet; the web downloads the file. Choose a trusted destination and protect the copy.

This release does not create a personal account, so there is no remote account to delete. Clearing app/site data or uninstalling the app also removes the local copy according to the operating system's behavior. This may lose the link to the pseudonymous session, so delete reports from Profile first. Any remaining report rows expire within 180 days.

Read the full Privacy policy.

Cloud personalization is not working

  • Confirm the device is connected to the internet.
  • Onboarding creates the first practice on the device and leaves cloud processing off.
  • To enable it later, open Profile and use the single cloud-processing control.
  • That control covers new optional scenes, dreams, images, translations and narrations.
  • Anthropic normally generates scene text and OpenAI is the failover; if neither is configured, approved Gemini processing may generate the scene.
  • Profiles marked Under 18 use only on-device alternatives.
  • If a provider is unavailable or a response cannot be used safely, Celeste keeps or creates the on-device alternative.

Voice or audio will not play

  • Check media volume and whether silent mode is interfering.
  • Press Play again; browsers may block automatic audio.
  • Try a narrator preview, which is bundled with the app and does not use the cloud.
  • For personal narration, confirm the single cloud-processing control in Profile and an internet connection.

A notification or alarm does not appear

  • The website cannot schedule a system reminder or alarm.
  • Open device settings and confirm notification permission.
  • Celeste Plan notices are ordinary reminders, not exact alarms. Battery saving, Focus modes or manufacturer restrictions may delay them.
  • Only the installed app on a compatible iPhone offers the system alarm with selected personal content: an affirmation, vision, Anchor Scene, dream phrase or personal phrase. It may request Alarm access. Android offers ordinary reminders only in this release, not that exact alarm.

Voice practice does not complete

  • The vision or Anchor Scene and the affirmation stay on screen while listening. Read the visible text; memorization is not required.
  • Tap to start the microphone and allow access when prompted by the system. A visible indicator shows when Celeste is listening.
  • Repeat the affirmation twice. The screen shows 1/2 after the first recognized reading and 2/2 when the practice is complete.
  • Practice uses only on-device recognition when available. If the device or language does not support it, permission is denied, or speech is not recognized, use the accessible manual completion option shown by the app.
  • Audio and transcripts are not stored. You may cancel, choose Not now or Snooze 10 min at any time; Celeste does not lock the phone.

The app is stuck

  1. Fully close and reopen Celeste.
  2. Check free device storage and install available updates.
  3. As a last resort, use Reset my journey or clear app data. This deletes local practice data and cannot be undone.

Privacy request or content report

For support, privacy questions or help with the available controls, email suporte@celestegroup.biz. Include only the information necessary to locate and fulfill the request.

On generated content, tap Report this AI content, choose a reason and submit the in-app form. Celeste sends the generated output, reason and optional note and minimum technical data; it does not include the prompt, your original answers or the raw dream report. A pseudonymous identifier authorizes submission and helps prevent abuse. Report rows are kept for no more than 180 days and can be deleted in Profile while this installation retains its session. Support cannot promise to locate a pseudonymous session lost after app-data clearing or uninstalling.

Safety and emergencies

Celeste is a reflection and well-being tool, not an emergency service, and does not diagnose or treat conditions. If you are in a crisis or immediate danger, contact local emergency services and a qualified person or professional.

Never send a password, API key, financial credential or identity document in a support request.

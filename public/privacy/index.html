Privacy policy

This policy explains how Celeste handles data on its website and in the application identified by package com.celesteapp.affirmations.

Updated September 4, 2026.

Controller's legal name awaiting confirmation. The store account owner must provide the exact legal name before public distribution. The confirmed public support and privacy contact is suporte@celestegroup.biz.

1. Summary

Personal practice data is primarily stored on the device. This release does not require a personal sign-in, offer an active subscription or purchase, contain ads, or use data for behavioral advertising.

Onboarding creates the first practice on the device and leaves cloud processing off. Cloud personalization features are optional and may be enabled later. They send only the content needed after an explicit choice and, where applicable, confirmation that the person is an adult. If permission is missing or a service is unavailable, Celeste keeps or creates an on-device alternative.

2. Data stored on the device

Depending on the features used, Celeste may store locally:

  • name, language, visual preference and onboarding answers;
  • manifestations, personal scenes, affirmations, dreams, private traces and earlier chapters;
  • favorites, practice history, streak and completed daily bridges;
  • on a compatible iPhone, the time and audio for the personal alarm content selected — an affirmation, vision, Anchor Scene, dream phrase or personal phrase; on Android, ordinary reminder times and days; the vision or Anchor Scene and affirmation selected for the Celeste Plan; and minimal practice receipts without recognized audio or transcripts;
  • personal images and narration audio in a private device cache.

This data provides continuity and is not published. A Community draft remains private when there is no authenticated account and no enabled community service.

3. Optional cloud processing

Celeste uses its backend as an intermediary and reduces each request to the content needed for the chosen feature. After onboarding, one adult control in Profile allows or prevents new optional requests for scenes, dreams, images, translations and neural narration.

Supabase Creates and maintains an anonymous technical session to authorize optional calls and enforce limits. It is not a personal sign-in in this release. It also keeps submitted AI-content reports in a restricted moderation queue.
Anthropic Receives selected answers and minimized context to generate personal scenes and Living Mirror chapters when that feature is authorized.
OpenAI May receive the same minimized scene-text request only as failover when the primary generation fails; it is not a second routine call.
Google Gemini Receives the content needed when a person requests a cloud translation, personal image or dream reflection. If Anthropic and OpenAI are not configured and approved Gemini processing is available, it may also generate the minimized scene text.
ElevenLabs Receives the final text, language and selected narrator to produce requested narration.
Vercel and BotID Host and protect the backend. They may process IP addresses, request identifiers, abuse signals and technical logs for security, fraud prevention and service operations.

Age range, gender, sexuality and names entered in dedicated fields for children, important people or a specific person are not included in scene-generation requests. Celeste also attempts to remove those names if they reappear in selected free-text answers; nevertheless, do not enter third-party data or highly sensitive information in free text.

For a new Living Mirror chapter, the prior scene and structured progress totals may be sent. Full Private Trace text and full dream reports are not part of that request. A dream contributes only its selected theme and feeling when that choice is enabled for the individual dream.

AI-content reports

When Report this AI content is used, Celeste sends to Supabase only the selected generated output of up to 4,000 characters (or a visual reference), the reason, an optional note of up to 500 characters, language, technical generation source, platform, app version and pseudonymous technical-session identifier. The prompt, original onboarding answers and raw dream report are not included. The report is kept in a private safety and moderation review queue. The identifier authorizes submission and helps rate-limit, deduplicate and prevent abuse. Report rows, including the identifier in those rows, are kept for no more than 180 days.

To remove them sooner, use Delete submitted AI-content reports in Profile. This deletes rows linked to the current pseudonymous identifier, but the anonymous technical session may remain for abuse prevention; it is not a personal account.

4. Voice, media, notifications and alarms

  • Pressing Play with permission sends the selected text, language and narrator to ElevenLabs. The returned audio is not published and may be kept in a private cache; items unused for 30 days are periodically removed.
  • Narrator previews are bundled with the app. They contain no onboarding answers and do not start a cloud generation request.
  • If dictation is used, speech recognition is supplied by the browser or operating system and may follow that provider's rules. Celeste receives the transcript, does not upload the raw recording itself, and sends text to Gemini only when cloud dream reflection is requested and authorized.
  • In the Celeste Plan, the vision or Anchor Scene and the affirmation remain visible in large text while the person reads; memorization is not required. The microphone starts only after a tap, and the affirmation is matched locally twice, with 1/2 and 2/2 progress. The app accepts only on-device recognition when the device and language support it. If they do not, permission is denied, or speech is not recognized, an accessible manual completion option is available.
  • Audio and the temporary transcript from this practice are not stored, sent to the backend, included in backups or written to logs. Celeste keeps only a minimal on-device receipt such as day, time slot, method and matching score, without the recognized text.
  • In the installed app, Celeste may request notification access. Only on a compatible iPhone does it offer the personal-content system alarm — for an affirmation, vision, Anchor Scene, dream phrase or personal phrase — and may request Alarm access; its time and audio stay on the device. Android offers ordinary reminders only in this release, not that exact alarm. Daily reminder copy is generic and does not expose a desire, dream, vision, Anchor Scene or affirmation on the lock screen. Celeste Plan notices are ordinary reminders, not exact alarms, and the operating system may delay them. Not now and Snooze 10 min remain available without speech; Celeste does not lock the device or make device access conditional on repetition.

5. Purposes, sharing and retention

Data is handled to:

  • deliver the practice and remember device preferences;
  • produce cloud content explicitly requested by the person;
  • play media, schedule ordinary reminders and, on a compatible iPhone, the personal-content alarm;
  • authorize requests, limit abuse, diagnose failures and protect the service.

No use for ads, data sales, cross-app tracking or advertising profiles was identified in this release's code. Data sent to providers is limited to the purposes above. Those providers may retain technical records or content under the contracts and terms that apply to the accounts used by Celeste.

External retention period to be confirmed. Exact retention periods for the active Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, ElevenLabs, Supabase, Vercel and BotID accounts depend on the owner's contracts and must be confirmed before public distribution. Celeste does not assume processing is ephemeral.

6. Controls and deletion

  • Turn cloud processing off in Profile to prevent new uploads.
  • Turn the Celeste Plan off to cancel its reminders; microphone permission can also be revoked in device settings.
  • Edit or delete manifestations, dreams and traces in their respective areas.
  • Use Delete submitted AI-content reports in Profile to remove remote rows linked to the current pseudonymous identifier before the 180-day limit. Do this before clearing app data or uninstalling because those local actions may remove access to the identifier used to locate the reports.
  • In Journey, Backup exports a readable, unencrypted JSON file with the local practice and local Community drafts. It excludes submitted reports, the pseudonymous reporting session, device consents, scheduled notifications and generated image files. The installed app opens the system share sheet; the web downloads the file. Protect the copy and choose only a trusted destination.
  • Use Profile → Reset my journey to remove the local practice, auxiliary records and private cache maintained by Celeste.
  • Clearing app/site data or uninstalling the app removes the local copy according to the operating system's behavior.

There is no personal account in this release, so there is no remote account to delete. If accounts are enabled, Celeste will publish an in-app and web deletion path before that feature is distributed.

7. Security

The public site and backend use HTTPS. Celeste applies session authorization, unified consent, usage limits, minimized requests, blocked unnecessary Android permissions and private device storage. No system is entirely risk-free; do not enter passwords, financial credentials, document numbers or another person's data.

8. Age and well-being notice

Profiles marked Under 18 remain on the on-device options and cannot authorize cloud personalization. The final store audience selection will be set by the account owner before distribution.

Celeste supports reflection and well-being. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat conditions, and is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal or financial support. In a crisis or immediate danger, contact local emergency services and qualified professionals.

9. Changes to this policy

The date at the top will change when this policy changes. Before accounts, payments, public Community features or a materially different use of data is enabled, Celeste must explain the change and present the required controls.
